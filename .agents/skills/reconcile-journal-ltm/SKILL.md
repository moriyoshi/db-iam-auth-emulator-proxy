# Reconcile journal and LTM

Read the complete journal, LTM index and topics, and TODO. Map durable journal claims to a topic or open task. Verify disputed claims against current code. Repair missing coverage with the smallest focused edits, leaving historical journal entries intact unless a factual correction needs an explicit dated note. Check index links and append a short reconciliation record. Do not copy session narration into LTM.
